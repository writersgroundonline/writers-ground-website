About Writer's Ground

Built for writers, by a writer.

Writer's Ground is a free resource for Australian and New Zealand writers looking to uncover opportunities, develop their craft, navigate publishing, and connect with a supportive community.

We focus on AU/NZ opportunities first, then expand to major international organisations worth knowing about. Everything is hand-curated — no AI-generated noise, no paywalls.
