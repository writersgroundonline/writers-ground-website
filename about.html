About Writer's Ground

Built for writers, by a writer.

Writer's Ground is a free resource for Australian and New Zealand writers — designed to help you uncover opportunities, develop your craft, navigate publishing, and connect with a community that gets it.

Why Writer's Ground exists.

Writers navigate an incredibly complex industry largely on their own.

Opportunities are scattered across websites, newsletters, journals, publishers, social media, and informal networks. Publishing pathways often feel opaque. Meaningful feedback can be difficult to access. Many talented writers struggle to get visibility, not because they lack ability, but because the system is fragmented, confusing, and difficult to break into.

At the same time, the future of writing is being reshaped. AI is changing how content is created, valued, and consumed. Traditional publishing is becoming more competitive. Writers are now expected not only to create strong work, but to market themselves, build audiences, understand the industry, and stand out in a world flooded with content.

Writer's Ground was created to fix that. It's a single, free, hand-curated database of opportunities that matter to writers at every stage — from those submitting their first short story to established authors seeking fellowships and residencies.

But that's not all. We aim to bring together opportunity discovery, resources, community, tools, feedback, mentorship, publishing insights, and industry transparency into one ecosystem -- a platform built specifically to help writers grow and succeed.

Meet our mascot — a wombat who knows every burrow worth exploring.

What we stand for.

🔍

Discover

We surface opportunities that writers might never find on their own — open submissions, competitions, prizes, fellowships, residencies, and more.

✍️

Develop

From free online courses to prestigious residencies, we help writers find the right programs to grow their craft at every stage of their journey.

🌱

Connect

Writing can be isolating. We're building a community where writers can share knowledge, support each other, and navigate the industry together.

🤝

Free, always

Access to information should never be a barrier to a writing career. The opportunities database is free to use, and will remain so.

🇦🇺

AU/NZ first

We prioritise opportunities that are relevant to Australian and New Zealand writers — because local context matters, and our literary community deserves its own spotlight.

🧭

Navigate publishing

The publishing industry is opaque. We aim to demystify it — from finding publishers that accept unsolicited manuscripts to understanding what agents actually want.

A note from the founder.

Writer's Ground is currently in its early stages. What you see now is the beginning — a free database of opportunities as a first step toward something much larger.

The full vision is a platform that truly serves writers: tools to track your submissions, a community forum, publishing guides, craft resources, and curated newsletters tailored to where you are in your writing journey. We're building it carefully, and we want writers to help shape it.

If you have feedback, suggestions, or just want to say hello — we'd love to hear from you. And if you want to be part of what's coming, join the waitlist.

— The Writer's Ground team
